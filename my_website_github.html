About

I completed my PhD at the Indian Institute of Technology Mandi in January 2026, working on deep learning methods for audio — specifically, building representations that stay reliable when the recording device, location, or acoustic conditions change. I currently work as a Tech Lead at HCLTech, where I build applied AI systems across computer vision, audio, and multimodal detection.

My research interests include Responsible AI, Explainable AI, Generative AI, and Multimodal AI.
